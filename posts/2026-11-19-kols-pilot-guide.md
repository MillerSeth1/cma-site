Welcome to Nogales International Airport (KOLS). The field sits about 7 miles northeast of Nogales, Arizona, in Santa Cruz County, roughly 42 nm south of Tucson. It's a public-use, non-towered airport near the U.S.–Mexico border, with a 7,200-foot runway at the foot of the Santa Cruz Valley.

We're Copper Mountain Air, the full-service FBO on the field. Whether you're stopping for fuel on a cross-country, clearing customs on the way north from Mexico, starting flight training, or moving freight that can't wait, this guide covers who we are and what you need to know before you fly in.

- **Fuel:** full-service Phillips 66 **Jet A** (including with additive) and **100LL Avgas**, dispensed by our line team. We're a Phillips 66 [WingPoints](https://www.wingpoints.com/) rewards program participant. Current Jet A and 100LL prices are posted on [AirNav](https://www.airnav.com/airport/KOLS). We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express and Discover.
- **FBO services:** aircraft ground handling, ramp and tie-down parking, and hangar leasing (call or email for current availability and rates). Our passenger terminal and pilot lounge has Wi-Fi, restrooms, coffee and refreshments, and a flight-planning desk, and we can help with hotel and rental-car arrangements and with catering. See [Fuel & FBO](../fuel-fbo.html).
- **Flight training:** FAA Part 61 single-engine flight training is available now at KOLS. Multi-engine training is arriving shortly, and FAA Part 141 flight school certification is in process. See [Flight Training](../flight-training.html).
- **Cargo:** on-demand expedited ground freight out of Nogales, plus air cargo coordination and ramp-to-truck handling on the field. Our fleet includes a Ford E-150 cargo van and a 26-foot dock-height Hino 268 box truck with a liftgate. See [Cargo](../cargo.html).

## Flying into KOLS

> **Not for navigation.** The figures below come from FAA airport data as published on AirNav (FAA information effective 29 October 2026). Always check the current Chart Supplement, NOTAMs and official charts before you fly.

### The field at a glance

| Item | KOLS |
|---|---|
| Elevation | 3,955 ft MSL (surveyed) |
| Runway | 4/22, 7,200 × 100 ft, asphalt |
| Runway lighting | Medium-intensity edge lights (MIRL), pilot-activated on CTAF |
| Visual glide slope / lights | 4-light PAPI on both ends (3.00° on Rwy 4, 4.00° on Rwy 22); REILs both ends |
| Traffic pattern | Left traffic, both runways |
| Displaced threshold | Rwy 22, 899 ft |
| Helipad | H1, 97 × 97 ft, concrete |
| Control tower | None (non-towered) |
| Time zone | UTC−7, Arizona does not observe daylight saving time |

| Service | Frequency |

The FAA remarks list Runway 22 as the calm-wind runway and note that wildlife may be on or near the airport. For IFR clearance, the FAA record lists a phone number for Tucson Approach; check the Chart Supplement for the current number.

### Instrument procedures

Published procedures include the **RNAV (GPS)-A** and **VOR-B** approaches and the **NOGALES THREE (OBSTACLE)** departure. Special alternate minimums and special take-off minimums apply, so review the full procedure set before filing.

## Arriving from Mexico: KOLS is an international airport of entry

FAA data lists KOLS as an international airport of entry. If you're arriving from Mexico or another foreign point, plan U.S. Customs and Border Protection requirements well ahead of your flight.

CBP's [General Aviation Airport Fact Sheet for KOLS](https://www.cbp.gov/sites/default/files/2026-02/KOLS%20GA%20Airport%20Fact%20Sheet%2020260224.pdf) (dated February 24, 2026) asks pilots to secure permission to land by contacting CBP before departing the foreign airport, and at least one hour before the expected arrival at KOLS. Permission is granted with a tolerance of 30 minutes either side of the ETA; if your arrival falls outside that window, you must contact CBP again. Park on the ramp at the Federal Inspection Station on the east side of the terminal, and be ready to present passports, visas, pilot certificate, medical certificate, aircraft registration and user fee decal. CBP office hours are 0800–1600 Mountain Time daily, and processing can be arranged 24/7 with at least one hour's notice: call 520-287-1433 during those hours, or 520-375-5785 after hours. The fact sheet notes a maximum passenger count of 20 at this location.

For FBO support on arrival, contact Copper Mountain Air at [520-287-9120](tel:+15202879120). Always follow current CBP instructions; this summary is not a substitute for official guidance.
