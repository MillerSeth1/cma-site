Nogales International Airport (KOLS) sits about 7 miles northeast of Nogales, Arizona, in Santa Cruz County, roughly 42 nm south of Tucson. It's a public-use, non-towered field near the U.S.–Mexico border, and Copper Mountain Air provides fuel and FBO services on the field.

> **Not for navigation.** The figures below come from FAA airport data as published on AirNav (FAA information effective 29 October 2026). Always check the current Chart Supplement, NOTAMs and official charts before you fly.

## The field at a glance

| Item | KOLS |
|---|---|
| Elevation | 3,955 ft MSL (surveyed) |
| Runway | 4/22, 7,200 × 100 ft, asphalt |
| Runway lighting | Medium-intensity edge lights (MIRL), pilot-activated on CTAF |
| Visual glide slope / lights | 4-light PAPI on both ends (3.00° on Rwy 4, 4.00° on Rwy 22); REILs both ends |
| Traffic pattern | Left traffic, both runways |
| Displaced threshold | Rwy 22, 899 ft |
| Helipad | H1, 97 × 97 ft, concrete |
| Control tower | None |
| Time zone | UTC−7, Arizona does not observe daylight saving time |

## Frequencies

| Service | Frequency |

| Nogales VOR/DME (OLS, on field) | 114.65 |

The FAA remarks list Runway 22 as the calm-wind runway and note that wildlife may be on or near the airport. For IFR clearance, the FAA record lists a phone number for Tucson Approach; check the Chart Supplement for the current number.

## Instrument procedures

Published procedures include the **RNAV (GPS)-A** and **VOR-B** approaches and the **NOGALES THREE (OBSTACLE)** departure. Special alternate minimums and special take-off minimums apply, so review the full procedure set before filing.
