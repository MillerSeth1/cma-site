Happy Thanksgiving from all of us at Copper Mountain Air, the full-service FBO at Nogales International Airport (KOLS).

On **Thursday, November 26, 2026**, we will have modified hours:

- **On site:** 7:00 a.m. to 12:00 p.m.
- **After 12:00 p.m.:** a staff member will be on call for fuel and other customer needs at [520-604-4020](tel:+15206044020).

Regular hours resume Friday, November 27: Monday–Saturday 7:00 a.m.–7:00 p.m., Sunday 7:00 a.m.–5:00 p.m.

We're grateful for our pilots, tenants, and neighbors in Nogales and Santa Cruz County. If you need fuel or other FBO support on Thursday morning, call us at [520-287-9120](tel:+15202879120). After 12:00 p.m., use the on-call number, [520-604-4020](tel:+15206044020). You can also email [operations@coppermountainair.com](mailto:operations@coppermountainair.com).
