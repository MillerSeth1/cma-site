If you want to learn to fly in Santa Cruz County, you can do it at Nogales International Airport (KOLS). Copper Mountain Air offers FAA Part 61 single-engine flight training on the field, with scheduling through [FlightCircle](https://www.flightcircle.com/v1/).

This page is an introduction to training here. For runway, frequency, and arrival details, see our [pilot's guide to KOLS](kols-pilot-guide.html).

## Why train at KOLS

KOLS is a non-towered county airport with a **7,200 × 100 ft** asphalt runway and a surveyed elevation of **3,955 ft MSL**. Density altitude is part of everyday flying in southern Arizona, especially on warmer days, so you learn performance planning from the start.

The field also sits close to useful practice airspace. Sierra Vista Municipal–Libby AAF (KFHU) offers nearby Class D practice, and Tucson International (KTUS) Class C is a short hop to the north. An on-field Nogales VOR/DME (OLS, 114.65) and published RNAV (GPS)-A and VOR-B approaches support instrument work as you move on. Airport data is from the FAA Chart Supplement and [AirNav for KOLS](https://www.airnav.com/airport/KOLS) (effective 29 October 2026 cycle).

The weather helps too. Using official ASOS observations from 2020 through 2025, KOLS averaged about **327 VFR days** a year during daytime hours (7:00 a.m. to 7:00 p.m. Arizona time), along with about 314 clear or sunny days. The method and monthly breakdown are on our [flight training](../flight-training.html#climatology) page.

## What we offer now — and what is still ahead

- **Part 61 single-engine training is available now**, including training toward the private pilot certificate.
- **Multi-engine training is arriving shortly** and is not offered yet.
- **FAA Part 141 flight school certification is in process.** Copper Mountain Air does not hold a Part 141 certificate at this time, and all current instruction is under Part 61.
- **On-demand charter is not offered.** An FAA Part 135 program is in progress and is not available yet.

Under [14 CFR 61.109](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-61.109), the FAA's minimum aeronautical experience for a private pilot certificate (airplane single-engine land) is 40 hours of flight time, with specific dual and solo requirements. Many students take more than the minimum. We will talk through a training plan that fits your schedule and experience. We do not list prices here; call or email us for current rates.

## Scheduling with FlightCircle

Copper Mountain Air uses [FlightCircle](https://www.flightcircle.com/v1/) for flight school scheduling and student accounts. Current students can sign in any time. If you are new, contact us first and we will get your account set up.

## How to get started

1. Call or email us to talk about training and availability.
2. We will help you take the next step, including FlightCircle access once you are ready to schedule.
3. Train at your own pace at KOLS, with cross-country flying across southern Arizona as you progress.
