Copper Mountain Air is the full-service fixed-base operator at Nogales International Airport (KOLS). We took on that role in **2024**, succeeding Tiffin Aviation, and we fuel and support aircraft on the field today.

For runway, frequencies, and customs arrivals, use the [pilot's guide to KOLS](kols-pilot-guide.html). This post covers fuel and ramp services.

## Fuel on the field

We dispense Phillips 66 Aviation **Jet A** (including with additive) and **100LL Avgas** by full-service truck-to-aircraft fueling. There is no self-serve fuel at KOLS.

Copper Mountain Air is a Phillips 66 [WingPoints](https://www.wingpoints.com/) rewards program participant. Current Jet A and 100LL prices are posted on [AirNav for KOLS](https://www.airnav.com/airport/KOLS); we do not list prices here.

If a fuel grade is temporarily unavailable, we post that on the [Fuel & FBO](../fuel-fbo.html) page and in the live [KOLS NOTAMs](../notams.html) list. Always confirm current NOTAMs with official FAA sources before you fly.

## Hours and how to pay

- **Hours:** Monday–Saturday 7:00 a.m.–7:00 p.m., Sunday 7:00 a.m.–5:00 p.m.
- **After-hours:** Call-out is available with prior arrangement, and a call-out fee may apply. Call [520-287-9120](tel:+15202879120) for details.
- **Payment:** Visa, Mastercard, American Express and Discover.

## Ramp, hangar and the FBO

Besides fuel, the FBO offers:

- Aircraft ground handling
- Ramp and tie-down parking — call or email for current rates
- Hangar leasing — call or email for current availability and rates
- A passenger terminal and pilot lounge with Wi-Fi, restrooms, coffee and refreshments, and a flight-planning desk
- Help with hotel and rental-car arrangements, and with catering

Let us know your ETA and fuel needs so the line team can be ready. You can [reserve a ramp spot or hangar](../contact.html?topic=FBO%20%2F%20ramp%20%2F%20hangar) or call us directly.
