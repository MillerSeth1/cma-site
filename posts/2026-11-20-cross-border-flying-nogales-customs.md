Nogales International Airport (KOLS) is a U.S. Customs and Border Protection **international airport of entry**, just north of the U.S.–Mexico border in Santa Cruz County, Arizona. If you are arriving from Mexico or another foreign point, plan the customs stop before you take off — and treat official CBP and FAA guidance as controlling.

This post summarizes publicly posted CBP requirements and the KOLS-specific fact sheet. It is not a substitute for a customs briefing, a regulation, or permission to land. For field data, frequencies, and instrument procedures, use our [pilot's guide to KOLS](kols-pilot-guide.html).

## What CBP expects on a general aviation arrival

CBP's [General Aviation Processing](https://www.cbp.gov/travel/general-aviation-processing) guidance and [arrival-and-departure fact sheet](https://www.cbp.gov/document/fact-sheets/general-aviation-arrival-and-departure-procedures) (February 2026) ask pilots of general aviation aircraft arriving from a foreign port or place to:

- **Transmit a compliant APIS manifest** for the flight and everyone on board, on time. For private aircraft, [19 CFR 122.22](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-19/section-122.22) generally requires that transmission no later than 60 minutes before departure from the foreign airport.
- **Secure explicit permission to land** from the CBP port of arrival, and do that **before departing** the foreign airport. CBP says pilots must contact the responsible port directly so they understand local procedures and port capacity.
- **Update CBP** if the already-transmitted manifest changes (for example, a tail-number change or a traveler substitution), and reconfirm permission to land.
- **Carry a current Private Aircraft User Fee decal**, purchased through CBP's [DTOPS](https://dtops.cbp.dhs.gov/) system, as noted in CBP's [general aviation FAQs](https://www.cbp.gov/travel/general-aviation-processing/general-aviation-processing-faqs).

KOLS is designated as an airport of entry under [19 CFR 122.13](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-19/section-122.13). That designation does not, by itself, complete your arrival requirements.

## KOLS-specific instructions

CBP's [General Aviation Airport Fact Sheet for KOLS](https://www.cbp.gov/sites/default/files/2026-02/KOLS%20GA%20Airport%20Fact%20Sheet%2020260224.pdf) (dated February 24, 2026) includes these local procedures. Confirm them against the current fact sheet before you fly, in case CBP has updated them.

- Secure permission to land by contacting CBP **before departing** the foreign airport, and at least **one hour** before the expected arrival at KOLS.
- Permission is granted with a tolerance of **30 minutes either side** of the ETA. If your arrival falls outside that window, you must contact CBP again.
- Park on the ramp at the **Federal Inspection Station on the east side of the terminal**.
- Be ready to present passports, visas, pilot certificate, medical certificate, aircraft registration and user fee decal.
- CBP office hours at this location are **0800–1600 Mountain Time daily**. Processing can be arranged 24/7 with at least one hour's notice: call **520-287-1433** during those hours, or **520-375-5785** after hours.
- The fact sheet notes a **maximum passenger count of 20** at this location.

Always follow current CBP instructions, [KOLS NOTAMs](https://www.notams.faa.gov/dinsQueryWeb/queryRetrievalMapAction.do?reportType=Report&retrieveLocId=KOLS&actionType=notamRetrievalByICAOs&submit=View+NOTAMs), and the FAA Chart Supplement.

## After you clear customs

Once CBP has released the aircraft, Copper Mountain Air can help with [fuel, ramp, tie-down and hangar](fuel-and-fbo-at-kols.html) needs at KOLS. Call [520-287-9120](tel:+15202879120) or email [operations@coppermountainair.com](mailto:operations@coppermountainair.com) with your ETA if you want fuel or parking after the inspection.

On-demand passenger charter is not offered at this time.
