Nogales sits right on the U.S.–Mexico border, and when a shipment can't wait for the next scheduled truck, speed matters. Copper Mountain Air works time-critical freight out of Nogales International Airport (KOLS) by air and by ground.

## Ground trucking

We run on-demand ground moves across Southern Arizona and beyond, including **on-demand expedited freight**.

### Ford E-150 Cargo Van

- Usable cargo length: 108 in (9 ft)
- Door opening width: approx. 55 in
- Max cargo height: 40 in
- Max payload: approx. 1,600 lbs

### Hino 268 Box Truck (26-foot dock-height)

- Cargo box length: 26 ft (dock-height)
- Liftgate for ground-level loading and delivery
- GVWR: 25,999 lbs (16,500 lbs empty weight)
- Max payload: up to 9,499 lbs theoretical (actual usable payload accounts for driver, fuel, equipment, and axle limits)

## Air cargo

At KOLS we coordinate air cargo and ramp handling for time-critical shipments. On-demand passenger charter is **coming soon** and is not offered yet.

## What to send us for a quote

- Pickup and delivery locations
- Ready time and required delivery time
- Pieces, dimensions and weight
- Any hazmat or special handling needs
