Nogales sits on the U.S.–Mexico border, and a lot of freight moving through Santa Cruz County is on a clock. When a shipment can't wait for the next scheduled truck, Copper Mountain Air handles time-critical freight out of Nogales International Airport (KOLS) by ground, and coordinates air cargo on the ramp.

We dispatch from the field at 11 Access Way, Nogales, AZ 85621. This is an overview of the ground operation; the [cargo](../cargo.html) page has the same fleet details and a quote form.

## Ground equipment

### Ford E-150 cargo van

Best for urgent hot-shot runs and smaller, time-critical shipments.

- Usable cargo length: 108 in (9 ft)
- Door opening width: approx. 55 in
- Max cargo height: 40 in
- Max payload: approx. 1,600 lb

### Hino 268 box truck (26-foot, dock-height)

For dock-to-dock moves and ground-level deliveries.

- Cargo box length: 26 ft (dock-height)
- Liftgate for ground-level loading and delivery
- GVWR: 25,999 lb (16,500 lb empty weight)
- Max payload: up to 9,499 lb theoretical (actual usable payload accounts for driver, fuel, equipment, and axle limits)

## What we move

- Warehouse, plant and dock pickups in Nogales, Rio Rico and Santa Cruz County
- On-demand expedited ground moves across southern Arizona and beyond
- Ramp-to-truck transfers at KOLS when freight arrives or departs by air

## What to send us for a quote

- Pickup and delivery locations
- Ready time and required delivery time
- Pieces, dimensions and weight
- Dock, liftgate or ramp handling needed
- Any hazmat or special handling needs
