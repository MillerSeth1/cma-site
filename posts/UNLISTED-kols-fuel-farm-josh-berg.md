Good fuel is the heartbeat of any airport. At Nogales International Airport (KOLS), a new fuel farm is taking shape, and we want to say thank you to one of the people making it happen: **Josh Berg, Assistant Project Manager at [KEAR Civil Corporation](https://kearcorp.com/)**.

## The project

The official name is the **Fuel Tank Replacement Project at Nogales International Airport**. Santa Cruz County owns the airport and the fuel tanks. C&S Engineers designed the project and is managing construction, and KEAR Civil Corporation is building it.

The County approved KEAR's $1,571,000 construction contract on October 15, 2025. Federal Aviation Administration Airport Improvement Program grants cover $1,185,000 of the work ($600,000 in entitlement funds plus a $585,000 Airport Infrastructure Grant), and ADOT Aeronautics is contributing as well. It's a real investment in general aviation for Santa Cruz County.

## The right builder for the job

KEAR has been based in Phoenix since 2001 and builds fueling systems at more than 50 commercial airports and military bases. In Arizona, that includes fuel farms at Phoenix-Mesa Gateway, Sedona and Phoenix Sky Harbor. KOLS is in experienced hands.

Josh has been managing the project day to day, coordinating the many moving parts it takes to replace an airport's fuel system. From all of us at Copper Mountain Air, the FBO at KOLS: thank you, Josh, for your work and professionalism.

## What's going in

The new tanks arrived on site around the end of September 2026. The system includes:

- **Jet A:** a 12,000-gallon Greer Steel tank with a floating suction, feeding a dispenser rated at 200 gallons per minute.
- **100LL Avgas:** a separate, dedicated avgas tank.

## What it means for pilots

When the new fuel farm comes online, pilots flying into KOLS can count on:

- Modern, purpose-built tanks for both Jet A and 100LL
- Leak monitoring built into the system
- Dependable fuel on the border, for everything from piston singles to turbine aircraft
